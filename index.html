About ChronoBible

One connected story, read in historical order

ChronoBible presents Scripture as a chronological journey through the historical settings in which the biblical story unfolds. The ten periods on this atlas gather books and writings by historical era so readers can follow the movement of the story from Creation and the Patriarchs to the life of the early Church.

The ChronoBible reading experience offers four chapters a day over 338 days, following the historical flow of events from Genesis to Revelation, including additional writings where indicated. ChronoBible is designed for offline reading: reading progress in the application stays on your device.

Period dates and groupings are presented as approximate historical guides in keeping with the established ChronoBible chronology.
